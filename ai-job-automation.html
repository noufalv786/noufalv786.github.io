AI Job Automation Platform

An AI-powered job search workflow designed to collect opportunities from multiple job sources, understand a candidate's existing CV, match and rank relevant roles, prepare tailored application documents, and streamline job applications.

01 · The problem

Job seekers often need to search several job portals separately, repeatedly review job descriptions, compare requirements with their CV, and prepare tailored application documents. This process can be time-consuming and difficult to manage consistently.

02 · The solution

A centralized AI-assisted workflow that brings job discovery, CV understanding, relevance matching, ranking, document preparation and application tracking into one process.

03 · Multi-source job discovery

The platform workflow collects job opportunities from the sources configured for the system, including:

LinkedInIndeedBaytNaukriGulf

Collected job records can be normalized into a common structure so that titles, companies, locations, descriptions and requirements can be compared consistently. Source access methods must comply with each platform's terms, permissions and technical restrictions.

04 · CV parsing & candidate profile

  • Process the candidate's existing CV.
  • Extract skills, experience, education and relevant keywords.
  • Build a structured candidate profile for downstream matching.
  • Use the profile to compare candidate experience with job requirements.

05 · AI matching & ranking

  • Analyze job descriptions and required qualifications.
  • Compare job requirements with the parsed CV profile.
  • Use semantic retrieval / vector search and LLM-based evaluation where configured.
  • Generate relevance scores and rank opportunities for review.

06 · Tailored application documents

  • Generate a job-specific CV version using relevant existing experience.
  • Draft a tailored cover letter aligned with the role.
  • Keep generated content grounded in the candidate's actual CV; do not invent skills, employers or achievements.

07 · Application automation

  • Organize selected jobs into an application workflow.
  • Support automated application submission where the source platform permits it.
  • Track application status and outcomes.
  • Use user review or approval where required by platform rules or the application process.
