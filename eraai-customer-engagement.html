AI-Driven Customer Engagement Platform

A unified business workspace bringing customer conversations, CRM activity, AI agents, campaigns, scheduling and reporting together in one dashboard.

AI AgentsCustomer EngagementCRMWhatsAppFull Stack

Original project dashboard screenshot supplied for this portfolio. The figures shown are dashboard values from the captured screen, not claims of measured business impact.

01 · The product problem

Customer engagement work can be spread across separate tools for conversations, customer records, campaigns, appointments and team activity. This platform brings those workflows into a shared workspace so teams can see and manage customer interactions centrally.

02 · The solution

A consolidated dashboard with navigation for AI, CRM, Inbox, Campaigns, Calendar, Reports, Favorites, Knowledge Base, Notifications, Team and Billing. The home view surfaces conversation, call, customer and daily activity summaries.

03 · What the dashboard demonstrates

  • AI Agent entry point for deploying and managing AI capabilities.
  • CRM area for customer and lead workflows.
  • Inbox area for WhatsApp conversations.
  • Campaign and calendar areas for outreach and meeting workflows.
  • Reports and dashboard summaries for customer and voice activity.
  • Knowledge Base, Team and Billing areas within the same workspace.

These are capabilities visible in the supplied screenshot; they do not by themselves establish which modules were personally implemented by me.
